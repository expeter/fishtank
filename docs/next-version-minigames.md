# Next-version planning: small ways to earn coins

Status: teaser only in version 1.0; agree on this plan before implementation.

Aquarium: Homework club. Choose German, maths, English or physics, then difficulty 1–10. Levels describe challenge difficulty, not school grades. A round lasts 1–3 minutes with three tasks, large touch answers and optional explanations; no countdown pressure, punishment or loss of money. German/English need explicit learning-language choices independent of interface language. Physics starts with everyday observations, then measurement and simple relationships. Curated, locally bundled tasks work offline. Define a reviewed curriculum and examples for every level before coding.

Coast: Nature helper mini-jobs. Short sorting, matching and spotting rounds about litter and habitats; feedback explains why an item belongs in a category. Do not teach children to handle glass/sharps: those are reported to an adult character. Existing casual shore cleanup remains optional and separate from these future paid rounds.

Shared design: one compact entry in the habitat menu; clear back/leave button; retain the aquarium state while playing. Choose a modest round reward only after simulating the economy against the roughly 20-hour late-species target. Avoid streak pressure, daily penalties or random paid rewards. Creative mode can play too, without requiring money. Save earned rewards exactly once per completed round, survive reload, and prevent repeated submissions.

Decisions for the planning discussion: age/knowledge examples for all ten levels, reward amount, task variety per level, language learning direction, visual feedback, and whether progress belongs to each world or the player. Test at 360px width, keyboard and screen reader; no overlapping panels or required text entry for young children.
